Ventra bridges legacy asset servicing (core banking, fund accounting, custodian ledgers) with modern tokenized DLT networks. It resolves the multi-billion-dollar post-trade fragmentation challenge by executing atomic Delivery-versus-Payment (DvP) with pre-trade statutory gate verification across jurisdictions and ledgers.

Multi-Rail DvP Engine

Orchestrates atomic settlement across Hyperledger Besu (IBFT2 private network), Canton Network, Arbitrum, and Solana with automated rollbacks and zero balance-sheet risk.

Statutory Jurisdiction Packs

Deterministic pre-flight rule engines codifying EU CSDR / MiCA / UCITS, US SEC 17a-4 / T+1 (Rule 15c6-1), and Singapore MAS Payment Services Act / Project Guardian.

Post-Quantum Record of Conformity

Append-only immutable audit trail using ML-DSA-65 (Dilithium3) signatures and SHA-256 hash chaining to provide zero-knowledge compliance proofs to supervisory authorities.

Multi-Portal Institutional Suite

Dedicated portals for Fund Administrators (NAV verification), Institutional Investors (order submission), and Financial Regulators (read-only audit, sub-second telemetry).
